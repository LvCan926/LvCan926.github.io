Can Lv (吕灿 in Chinese) is currently a first-year M.S. student at the [School of Artificial Intelligence](https://iai.buaa.edu.cn/), [Beihang University (BUAA)](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Shiji Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Do5jf8oAAAAJ&hl=en). He received his bachelor's degree from the School of Computer Science, [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT)](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/). He is currently an AI intern at Unity and was previously an AI intern at Huawei.

His research focuses on
* Agentic Reinforcement Learning (AgenticRL)
* Agent Harness
* Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI)
